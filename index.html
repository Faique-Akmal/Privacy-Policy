1. Introduction

Lantern 360 is a location-tracking and field management application developed by COWBERRY Industries Pvt. Ltd. to help organizations track employee movements during official work hours for safety, transparency, and performance purposes. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your data is handled securely and transparently.

By using the Lantern 360 app, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to provide core features of the app:

a) Location Data

  • Precise Location (GPS): Collected in real-time while you are on duty, during travel start/stop events, and periodically (every few seconds or minutes) to record movement history.
  • Background Location: Collected only when the employee has marked themselves as “on duty” or “travel started.”
  • Approximate Location: May be used when GPS is weak or unavailable.

b) Camera / Media

Selfie capture: Used for identity verification at check-in, check-out, and hourly attendance verification.

c) Device Information

Device model, OS version, app version, IP address, unique device ID, and crash logs — for security and troubleshooting.

d) Usage Logs

Activity timestamps (check-in/out, travel start/stop, pause/resume, location updates), network status, and battery level to ensure proper functioning of tracking.

We do not collect personal content, contact lists, or messages.

3. Purpose of Data Collection

We collect and use data for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • Attendance verification and route monitoring.
  • Employee safety during field operations.
  • Reporting and payroll validation.
  • Preventing fraud and misuse (e.g., fake check-ins).
  • App analytics, debugging, and improvement.

4. How We Store and Protect Your Data

  • All data is transmitted securely over HTTPS (TLS encryption).
  • Location and selfie data are stored on secure cloud servers (India-based) managed by authorized service providers.
  • Access to data is restricted to authorized HR/Admin personnel only.
  • We retain location and attendance data for a maximum of 90 days, after which it is automatically deleted or anonymized unless legally required.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share limited information:

  • With your employer’s HR/Admin dashboard (for attendance, safety, and billing purposes).
  • With trusted service providers (e.g., Firebase for analytics, Google Maps for route visualization).
  • With law enforcement if legally required.

All third parties are bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations.

6. Permissions Used in the App

  • Location (Foreground & Background): To track travel and job movement only during duty hours.
  • Camera: For capturing selfies during attendance verification.
  • Storage: To store and upload images temporarily.
  • Network Access: For syncing data with the secure cloud server.

The app requests permissions only when necessary, and you can revoke them anytime from device settings.

7. User Rights and Controls

You have the right to:

  • Access and review your stored data.
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate data.
  • Withdraw consent for tracking (though this may limit app functionality).

Contact us at customercare@cowberry.world for any privacy-related requests.

8. Data Retention Policy

Location & activity data: Retained for up to 90 days.
Selfie/attendance images: Retained for 30 days.
After this period, all identifiable data is permanently deleted or anonymized.

9. Children’s Privacy

Lantern 360 is designed for workplace use only and is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
